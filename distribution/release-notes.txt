Dune Companion: a game companion for Dune: Imperium. Fan-made, not affiliated with Dire Wolf or Legendary.

You need two devices: a phone running this app (the "display", which lies on the table) and any other phone, tablet or laptop with a browser (the "admin panel").

First run:
1. Install and open Dune Companion on the display phone. Allow notifications so it keeps running.
2. Put the other device on the same Wi-Fi as the display phone and scan the QR code on the display. If the page won't load (guest or office Wi-Fi often blocks it), turn on the display phone's hotspot, join it from the other device, and scan again.
3. On the panel, open the GUIDE tab. It explains setup, the gestures (spin, shake, tap, letters), scoring and stats.
4. Turn the display phone's media volume up. The companion speaks.

Stats for every finished game are kept on the display phone. Use EXPORT HISTORY on the STATS tab to back them up.
