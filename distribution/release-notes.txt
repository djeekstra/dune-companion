Dune Companion 0.2

Optional finished-game stats sharing on DubbleG:
- Ask the DubbleG admin for a phone credential from Stats devices.
- In the browser panel's game setup, choose CONNECT DUBBLEG, then enter the credential on the display phone.
- Check SHARE FINISHED STATS ON DUBBLEG before starting to share that game's results when a winner is recorded.
- You can also share a past game from STATS. Copy its unlisted link from the game's stats page.

Games stay local until you choose to share them. Anyone with the link can view them.
Keep the app open with internet access for pending uploads; reopening resumes retries.
The DubbleG backend must support Stats devices before connecting.

Start a game by scanning the display's QR code with a second phone on the same Wi-Fi
or hotspot. Spin the display phone flat to end an agent turn, shake to reveal, or
double-tap to end an agent turn, or triple-tap to reveal / complete combat. Long-press for debug/exit. The panel's GUIDE has full instructions.
