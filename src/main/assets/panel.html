Dune Companion is a game companion for Dune: Imperium. One phone, the display, lies face up in the middle of the table. It shows whose turn it is, times each turn, and announces handovers and phases out loud. This page is the admin panel, open on any other phone, tablet or laptop, for setup, scoring and corrections.

SETUP

  • Open Dune Companion on the display phone and allow notifications (it keeps the companion running with the screen off).
  • Connect this device to the same Wi-Fi as the display phone, then scan the QR code on the display. If the page won't load (guest or office Wi-Fi often blocks it), turn on the display phone's hotspot, join it from this device, and scan again.
  • Turn the display phone's media volume up. The voice comes from its speaker.
  • On the GAME tab, pick 3 or 4 players, set each seat's colour clockwise from any seat, and add names if you like. Two people can share a colour. Choose the first player and check the turn order shown, then press START GAME.
  • Lay the display phone face up in the middle of the table.

ON THE DISPLAY PHONE

  • Spin it a full turn while it lies flat to end an agent turn. Two half-turns the same way also count. Let it rest for a second first. A spin as you set it down doesn't count.
  • Shake it in the hand to finish your reveal turn. During combat, a shake marks combat resolved.
  • Double-tap the screen to end an agent turn. Triple-tap to finish your reveal or resolve combat. Pause briefly after tapping; the app waits for the last tap before acting.
  • Draw a letter on the screen to claim a space for whoever has the turn: S Swordmaster, C High Council, M Mentat. Experimental: S and C read well, M less so. If a letter isn't recognised, nothing happens.
  • Long-press the display for the gesture debug overlay and the exit buttons.

A ROUND

  • The companion says the round and who is first player, then names whoever has the turn after each turn ends. Players who have revealed are skipped, and the companion says so.
  • Turn timers call out at 5, 7 and 10 minutes, and it says each hour of game time.
  • When the last player reveals, combat starts by itself. When combat is resolved, shake the display or press COMPLETE COMBAT. That is the only step anyone has to take between rounds.
  • Collect your rewards, Makers and Recall then run by themselves, and the next round starts. The display and this panel count down to each one ("MAKERS IN 12") so nobody sits waiting for a button.
  • Victory points are asked for after conflicts 5 and 9, and at the end. The companion says "Update the scores" and the round waits until they're entered here. Once saved, it reads the standings back, leader first. REPLAY CUE repeats the reminder.
  • After conflict 10, enter the final scores and pick the winner. To end early, use a WINS button under Declare Winner.

FIXING MISTAKES

  • UNDO TURN gives the last turn back (for a spin or shake that fired by accident).
  • UNDO ROUND undoes the last round change. END ROUND skips to the next round.
  • PAUSE TIMER stops the turn and game clocks for breaks. Paused time isn't counted in stats.
  • Swordmaster and High Council toggles are under SWORDMASTER · HIGH COUNCIL. Tap again to take one back.

STATS

To share a finished game, choose CONNECT DUBBLEG in game setup and enter the one-time phone credential on the display. Check SHARE FINISHED STATS ON DUBBLEG before starting to share when a winner is recorded. You can also open a past game here and choose SHARE ON DUBBLEG. This uploads names, scores and turn timings and returns an unlisted link. Anyone with the link can view it. Uploads retry while the display app is running with internet access. Deleting local history does not delete a shared page; ask the DubbleG admin to revoke it.

Every game that ends with a winner is saved on the display phone. The STATS tab shows the current game live and every past game: game time, turns and time per player, agent vs reveal turns, time per round, and the victory points at each checkpoint. ALL GAMES totals wins and averages by player name, so type names the same way each time. Use EXPORT HISTORY now and then as a backup.

TROUBLESHOOTING

  • Panel says NO CONNECTION: check both devices are on the same network, or use the hotspot. The display app must still be running (look for its notification).
  • No voice: raise the display phone's media volume, then press TEST SPEAKER.
  • Closed the app by accident: reopen it. The game is saved on every change and carries on where it was.
  • To close it: long-press the display, then EXIT · KEEP GAME (the clocks pause, and reopening carries on; resume here with RESUME TIMER) or EXIT · END GAME (tap twice; the game is discarded). Stop on its notification keeps the game.
